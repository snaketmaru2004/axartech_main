# Professional Design Upgrades - AxarTech

## Overview
Complete professional design overhaul focusing on enterprise-grade aesthetics, sophisticated typography, refined color palette, and premium user experience.

## Color Palette Refinement
- **Primary**: `#4573F5` (219 92% 46%) - Professional deep blue
- **Secondary**: `#9D5DFF` (278 90% 46%) - Elegant purple
- **Foreground**: `#0F1419` (218 14% 7%) - Deep charcoal for contrast
- **Borders**: Subtle grays with refined opacity levels
- **Shadows**: Professional elevation system (1-4 levels)

## Typography System
- **Display Font**: Poppins (Bold, Semibold) - Headlines & CTAs
- **Body Font**: Inter (Regular, Medium) - Content & descriptions
- **Font Classes**:
  - `.font-display` - Bold headlines with tight tracking
  - `.font-headline` - Semibold subheadings
  - `.font-body` - Regular content with relaxed leading

## Shadow Elevation System
Four-level professional shadow system:
- **elevation-1**: Subtle shadows for cards (0 1px 3px)
- **elevation-2**: Medium elevation for interactive elements
- **elevation-3**: Strong elevation on hover
- **elevation-4**: Maximum elevation for modals
